# Neo Audit of .koto/ Content - 2026-04-23

Systematic review of all agents, flows, and rules against ai-engineering standards.

## Audit Scope

- **Agents reviewed**: 8 (Bella, Kai, Levi, Mika, Minion, Neo, Noah, Rio)
- **Flows reviewed**: 3 (development, fix-issue, review-pr)
- **Rules reviewed**: 5 (ai-engineering, code-review, git-workflow, issue-quality, rust-developer)
- **Guide reviewed**: 1 (Guide.md)

## Methodology

Each component evaluated against principles from:
- Chip Huyen "AI Engineering" (O'Reilly 2025)
- Anthropic "Building Effective Agents"
- .koto/rules/ai-engineering.md

Focus areas:
1. **Agent prompts**: Role clarity, output format specification, few-shot examples, prompt length
2. **Flow design**: Step decomposition, compound error risk, validation/reflection, conditional logic
3. **Rules**: Actionability, examples vs. descriptions, anti-patterns, domain specificity

## Findings Summary

### Agents

**Strengths**:
- All agents have clear, focused roles (except Rio)
- Good use of explicit instructions in most prompts
- Appropriate model selection (Opus for architects, Haiku for simple tasks)

**Issues identified**: 4
- Missing explicit output format specifications (#64)
- No few-shot examples in any agent prompts (#65)
- Rio violates single responsibility (dual role) (#66)
- Neo prompt risks lost-in-middle at 150 tokens (#70)

### Flows

**Strengths**:
- Clear step decomposition and dependencies
- Good use of input/output chaining
- Appropriate use of parallelization in review-pr

**Issues identified**: 4
- Missing validation steps between design and execution (#67)
- No reflection/error correction mechanisms (#67)
- Conditional logic embedded in fix-issue ship step (#68)
- review-pr parallel vs. sequential trade-off not evaluated (#71)
- Verbose formatting instructions in review-pr consensus (#72)

### Rules

**Strengths**:
- ai-engineering.md is comprehensive, well-sourced, actionable
- Clear anti-pattern sections in most rules
- Concise, bullet-point format throughout

**Issues identified**: 1
- Missing few-shot examples in code-review, issue-quality, rust-developer (#69)

## Conclusion

The .koto/ content demonstrates solid AI engineering fundamentals. The issues identified are primarily about optimization and refinement rather than fundamental problems:

- **Prompt engineering**: Missing few-shot examples and explicit output formats (standard gap in initial implementations)
- **Flow design**: Lack of validation/reflection steps increases compound error risk

No breaking issues found. All agents and flows are functional and follow core principles. The improvements will enhance reliability and output quality.
